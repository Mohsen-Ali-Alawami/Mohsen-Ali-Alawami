# 👋 Welcome

Thank you for visiting my GitHub profile.

I am an **Assistant Professor** in the **Division of Computer Engineering** at **Hankuk University of Foreign Studies (HUFS), South Korea**.

My research focuses on designing intelligent and secure computing systems through the integration of **Artificial Intelligence**, **Cybersecurity**, and **Computer Networks**.

I am passionate about developing practical AI-driven solutions for authentication, digital identity, network security, IoT systems, deepfake detection, and explainable AI.
